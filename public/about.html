"Education is not the learning of facts, but the training of the mind to think."

Established in 2011 by Founder & Director Sagar Sir, Sagar Classes has grown into one of Thane's premier educational coaching institutes. Our core philosophy is to provide rigorous academic training combined with individual mentorship, helping every child achieve their highest potential across SSC and HSC board curriculums.
